Our story

Serving the HVAC-R industry since 1991

Ajanta Electric & Refrigeration Co. — known across the region as AC वाला (#A Freezing World) — has grown into a complete cooling and heating solutions provider based in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad), Maharashtra.

Our business spans trading, system design and turnkey project execution for all types of HVAC-R equipment. Over the years we have gradually developed deep expertise and now deal in the entire range of HVAC-R products — from residential air-conditioners to industrial cold chain and heat-pump systems.

We maintain one of the largest ready stocks of material in the region, backed by an experienced project-execution team and strong application & technical support.
